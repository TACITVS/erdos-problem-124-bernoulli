Mathematical advance:
  Isolated the Brown/complete-sequence interval criterion in the form needed
  by the scaled-power conductor route.

Core lemma:
  If a seed represents [L,U] and ordered positive terms b_i satisfy

    b_i <= U - L + 1 + sum_{j<i} b_j,

  then after adjoining those terms the represented interval is

    [L, U + sum_i b_i].

Central-conductor consequence:
  If the seed total is S0 and its central conductor is c, then the seed
  interval is [c+1, S0-c-1].  A complete finite scaled block extends this to

    [c+1, S1-c-1]

  where S1 is the new total.  Thus the conductor bound c is preserved.

Proof route impact:
  The scaled middle-interval theorem now has a smaller sufficient-condition
  target: choose quotient-block terms whose increasing order is complete over
  an existing central interval.

Status:
  This does not prove the global scaled middle-interval theorem.  It certifies
  a reusable algebraic sublemma and turns part of the theorem into an ordered
  inequality problem on scaled terms.
