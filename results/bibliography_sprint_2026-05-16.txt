Conclusion:
  Yes.  The current proof audit has three open obligations:
    1. global residue saturation;
    2. post-saturation central interval formation;
    3. global explicit analytic bounds for independent base pairs/classes.

  The bibliography identifies modern complete-sequence, exponential-sequence,
  subset-sum, and Diophantine-approximation sources that plausibly attack those
  obligations.  The next proof-design step should use those sources before
  committing to a bespoke ResidueGateCertificate API.

Highest-priority sources:

    Acta Arithmetica 77 (1996), 133-138.
  - Bergelson and Simmons, "A note on a theorem of Erdos and Graham",
    arXiv:1507.02208.
  - Chen, Fang, and Hegyvari, "On complete sequences", 2016.
  - Xue, Fang, and Ma, "On exponential type sequences", Discrete Applied
    Mathematics 338 (2023), 86-98.
  - Yu, Chen, and Chen, "A generalization of the Erdos-Birch theorem",
    European Journal of Combinatorics 128 (2025), Article 104187.
  - Mignotte-Waldschmidt and Laurent linear-forms sources for explicit
    independent-pair tail thresholds.
