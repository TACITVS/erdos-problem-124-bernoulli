  A residue frame of width R modulo m plus a divisible block G = mG' whose
  quotient has conductor c' gives

    c(F union G) <= m(c' + 1) + R - 1

  whenever the lifted interval reaches the half-sum of the whole finite seed.

  Power-saving conductor bounds now transfer across a fixed residue frame:

    c(G') = O(T^(1-epsilon))  =>  c(F union mG') = O(T^(1-epsilon))

  up to constants, provided the half-sum reach condition holds.
