Mathematical advance:
  Proved an algebraic residue-saturation lemma.

Statement:
  If gcd(a,m)=1, then the powers

    a^k, a^(k+h), ..., a^(k+(m-2)h)

  where h is the multiplicative order of a modulo m, form a complete residue
  frame modulo m.  They are all congruent to the same unit a^k mod m, so their
  subset sums cover 0, u, 2u, ..., (m-1)u.

Conductor route:
  In any modulus where one available base is a unit, residue saturation is not
  a serious obstruction.  The remaining task is to prove a conductor or ray
  theorem for the divisible block and then lift it through the residue frame.

Status:
  This is a genuine algebraic lemma, but it does not prove Erdős 124.  It
  removes one class of residue-search obligations and sharpens the modular
  induction route toward the power-saving central conductor theorem.
