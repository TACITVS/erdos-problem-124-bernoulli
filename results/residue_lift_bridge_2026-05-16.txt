Mathematical advance:
  Added an interval version of the residue bridge.

Statement:
  If a finite frame F has subset-sum representatives rho_r for every residue
  modulo m, with R = max rho_r, and a disjoint block G represents every
  multiple of m in [M0,M1], then F union G represents every integer in

    [M0 + R, M1].

Conductor route:
  If G = m G' and G' has conductor c', the lifted interval starts at

    m(c' + 1) + R.

  Therefore the power-saving central conductor theorem can be attacked by
  proving power-saving bounds for residue-frame width R and for the scaled
  conductor c' in a suitable divisible block.

Status:
  This does not prove the global theorem.  It creates a typed modular-induction
  bridge for the remaining conductor-growth problem.
