Bounded-gap bridge

Mathematical advance:
  Proved and encoded the bridge:

    seed interval [M, M + H]
    + unbounded tail subset sums with consecutive gaps <= G
    + G <= H + 1
    => all integers >= M are represented.

Correction:
  Bounded tail gaps plus complete residue representatives is not enough as a
  theorem target.  The bridge needs either an actual seed interval of sufficient
  length or a stronger tail theorem such as a full arithmetic progression.

Status:
  The bridge is certified as an arithmetic reduction.  Except for the binary
  powers smoke test, the executable rows are conditional capacity checks: they
  record how small a tail gap bound would need to be for the current seed
  interval to imply a ray.  The global proof still needs a theorem that
  produces seed intervals and Chen-Fang-Hegyvari style tail gap bounds for
  arbitrary admissible base sets.
