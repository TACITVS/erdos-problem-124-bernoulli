Extracted usable input:
  - Chen-Fang-Hegyvari Lemma 2.1 gives a bounded-gap criterion for subset sums:
    eventual domination of each next term by the previous partial sum plus a
    fixed buffer implies bounded gaps in P(B).
  - The 2016 density theorem for S_p A_t warns that positive density is weaker
    than completeness.
  - The 2016 Fibonacci proof gives a residue-to-completeness template:
    finite residue representatives, periodicity modulo d, base-p expansion.
  - The accessible 2023 preview defines a quasi-complete residue witness as a
    p-element finite set whose gcd-normalization is a complete residue system
    modulo p. Exact theorem imports beyond the exposed statements still require
    the full article.
