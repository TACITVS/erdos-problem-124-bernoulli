Mathematical advance:
  Isolated the exact p-adic condition for a base to contribute to an
  m-divisible quotient block.

Core criterion:
  If m = product p^alpha_p, then m divides d^e if and only if

    e * v_p(d) >= alpha_p

  for every p dividing m.

  Hence d has an eventually m-divisible tail if and only if every prime
  divisor of m divides d.  The first exponent at floor k is

    max(k, max_p ceil(alpha_p / v_p(d))).

Proof route impact:
  Quotient-block selection now has a typed arithmetic front end.  Invalid
  choices are rejected for precise reasons:
    - the frame base is not a unit modulo m;
    - a quotient base is missing a modulus prime.

Status:
  This does not solve quotient-block selection globally.  It proves the
  p-adic well-formedness criterion and leaves the additive-strength choice of
  m,u,d_i as the next hard step.
