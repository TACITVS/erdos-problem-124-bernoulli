If E_2 / 2^N grew only as polylog(T), the Evertse-Schlickewei bound on
S-unit equations would imply the strict conductor theorem.  Empirically
E_2 / 2^N grows roughly as T^0.4, so the polylog hypothesis fails: the
energy method reduces to LLT-level off-resonance bounds, not bypassing them.
