Mathematical advance:
  Composed p-adic quotient selection, complete-sequence absorption, and modular
  conductor lift into one typed bridge.

Core statement:
  Given a valid quotient selection modulo m, an existing quotient central
  conductor c', and selected quotient terms satisfying the complete-sequence
  inequalities, the quotient interval becomes a ScaledCentralBlock with the
  same conductor c'.  If residue lifting reaches the whole half-sum, the
  ordinary conductor bound is

    m(c' + 1) + R - 1

  where R is the residue-frame width.

Proof route impact:
  The bridge exposes two exact remaining failure modes:
    - selected quotient terms do not touch the current quotient interval;
    - the lifted interval does not reach the whole half-sum.

Status:
  This does not solve the global quotient-block-selection or half-sum-reach
  theorems.  It makes their interface executable and algebraic.
