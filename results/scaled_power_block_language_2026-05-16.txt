  Made quotient blocks precise as finite unions of scaled progressions q*d^n.

Core normalization:
  If m divides d^k, then

    d^(k+j) / m = (d^k / m) * d^j.

  Thus an m-divisible pure-power tail becomes a scaled progression after
  quotienting.
